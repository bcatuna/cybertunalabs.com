1. Who we are

Cyber Tuna Labs LLC (“Cyber Tuna Labs,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a Nevada limited liability company located in Las Vegas, Nevada, United States. We operate the game Swarm Genesis.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with Swarm Genesis, including the current free web preview on itch.io and, when they exist, future Apple App Store and Google Play builds.

2. Scope and current status

This policy covers:

  1. The Web / itch.io Preview Build that is publicly available today as a free, in-development HTML5 game at https://cybertunalabs.itch.io/swarm-genesis; and
  2. Planned Mobile Builds for the Apple App Store and Google Play, described in Section 5. Those builds are not live as of the effective date. Section 5 is marked (not yet active — applies only if and when mobile store builds launch) and does not describe current data practices.

As of November 25, 2026, Swarm Genesis does not include player accounts, cloud save to a Cyber Tuna Labs server, a studio game backend, third-party analytics SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, advertising SDKs, or real-money payment SDKs. In-game store items in the public preview are simulated (TEST) and charge no real money.

3. The short version (current Web / itch.io Preview Build)

  • The operator is Cyber Tuna Labs LLC, not an individual person acting in a personal capacity.
  • We do not currently ask you for your name, email, or payment card through the game, and the game client does not currently integrate third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or payment SDKs.
  • Game progress is saved on your device or in your browser, not on a Cyber Tuna Labs server. You can manually export and import a JSON save file.
  • The web build is hosted on itch.io. itch.io is a third-party platform. Loading and playing the game in a browser involves network requests to deliver the game files, and itch.io may set cookies or otherwise process information under itch.io’s Privacy Policy. We do not claim that the web preview “plays fully offline” or makes “no network calls.”
  • In-game store items in the preview are labeled TEST (or equivalent) and charge no real money. Apple App Store and Google Play billing are not active in the current web preview.
  • A “Send Feedback” control opens the public itch.io game page so you can use itch.io’s comments. That is navigation to itch.io, not a Cyber Tuna Labs telemetry pipeline.

4. Information handling — Web / itch.io Preview Build (current)

4.1 Information you provide

  • We do not currently require an account, and the game does not currently collect your name, email address, or payment information.
  • If you email us (for example, a privacy request or a support question), we will receive whatever you include in that message and will use it to respond and to keep a record of the request as needed.
  • If you use the in-game Send Feedback control, your browser opens https://cybertunalabs.itch.io/swarm-genesis. Any comment, account activity, or cookie on that page is governed by itch.io, not by a Cyber Tuna Labs comment system.

4.2 Game progress and local storage

Your progress (including soft currencies, upgrades, settings, local personal records, and similar gameplay state) is stored locally:

  • On native or desktop builds, in the game’s user-data folder (user://), including the save file (for example, save.json) and any file you export (for example, swarm-genesis-save.json).
  • On the web build, the engine persists user:// data in the browser (typically via IndexedDB), and the game also keeps a synchronous copy of the save JSON in the browser’s localStorage so progress is less likely to be lost if a tab closes before the IndexedDB write finishes. A small local diagnostic sidecar may also be written to localStorage. These local copies are not uploaded to a Cyber Tuna Labs server.
  • You may manually export and import a JSON save file. An exported save is a file on your device that you control. We do not receive a copy unless you choose to send one to us (for example, attached to a support email).

Browser storage can be cleared, blocked, or treated as ephemeral (for example, in some embedded iframe or third-party storage situations, including some in-app browsers). Clearing site data, using a private window, or uninstalling the game can delete your progress. We do not operate a cloud backup and do not warrant that local progress will survive browser, device, or version changes. See also the Terms of Service.

4.3 Network activity on the web preview

The web build must be downloaded and run in your browser from itch.io’s hosting. That involves ordinary network requests to itch.io (and any content-delivery systems itch.io uses) to fetch the game, and may involve itch.io cookies, logs, and account features if you are logged into itch.io. Those practices are governed by itch.io’s Privacy Policy and Terms of Service, not by Cyber Tuna Labs.

In the current preview build, the game client does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or payment SDKs, and it does not upload your save data to a Cyber Tuna Labs server. Opening Send Feedback or following an external link uses your browser to navigate to the linked page.

4.4 In-game store in the preview build

The preview build includes a simulated store catalog (including items such as Remove Ads, a Starter Pack, a seasonal Flight Pass, and Flux packs) for design and playtesting. Purchase confirmations are labeled TEST (or equivalent). No real money is charged, and no payment card numbers are collected. Apple App Store and Google Play billing are not active in the current web preview.

4.5 How we use information today

  • Local save data is used only to operate the game on your device (resume progress, remember settings, and keep local personal records).
  • Email you send us is used to respond to you and to administer privacy or support requests.
  • We do not currently use game data for advertising or analytics.

4.6 Sharing today

We do not sell your personal information. We do not currently share game-progress data with advertisers or analytics providers.

itch.io, as the host of the web preview, may process information as an independent platform operator when you visit or play on itch.io. If we are legally required to disclose information (for example, in response to valid legal process), we will do so only to the extent required.

5. Planned Mobile Builds — Apple App Store & Google Play

Future Mobile Releases (Not Yet Active): Swarm Genesis is currently available only as a free web preview on itch.io. The mobile-store practices below are not yet active and will apply only if and when iOS or Android builds with in-app purchases or advertising are published.

As of November 26, 2026, Swarm Genesis is not yet available on the Apple App Store or Google Play. We include this section for transparency so players know how planned mobile features will be handled once released.

When a practice below is activated, we will update the effective date of this policy, publish the updated policy at the public URL, bundle the updated text in-game, and complete required platform disclosures (including Apple App Privacy and Google Play Data Safety) before that build ships.

5.1 In-app purchases (IAP)

If you make a purchase in an Apple App Store or Google Play build, payment is processed by Apple or Google, as applicable. Cyber Tuna Labs LLC receives purchase entitlements, product identifiers, and receipts or similar purchase-validation data sufficient to deliver and restore what you bought. We never receive your payment card number, full billing address, or other payment-method details. Apple’s and Google’s privacy policies, terms, and refund rules govern the payment transaction itself.

As currently designed:

  • Non-consumable purchases (for example, Remove Ads and the Starter Pack) should be restorable through a Restore Purchases control.
  • Purchased Flux (premium virtual currency) is designed not to expire.
  • Seasonal entitlements such as a Flight Pass may be limited to a stated season. That is an entitlement window, not expiry of purchased Flux, and it will be disclosed at the time of purchase.

5.2 Rewarded video advertising (for example, Google AdMob)

The current design, if activated, is optional rewarded video ads only (for example, a revive, double-scrap, Flux-drop, or daily-crate bonus). Forced or interstitial ads are not part of the current design.

If Google AdMob or a similar advertising SDK is integrated, that provider may process information such as:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (for example, IDFA on iOS if you grant permission, and GAID or app-set ID on Android)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from IP
  • Device and app information (device type, OS version, app version)
  • Ad performance and interaction data (impressions, clicks, completed rewarded views)
  • Consent or preference signals (Apple’s App Tracking Transparency, Google’s User Messaging Platform / IAB consent signals)

Personalized advertising may involve the advertising SDK using this information to serve or measure ads. We will not describe advertising as “no tracking” if an identifier-based ad SDK is in the build.

Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT) — iOS. If a mobile iOS build uses an advertising or tracking SDK that requires it, we will present Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt before tracking you across apps or websites owned by other companies. If you deny permission, we will request that ads be served in a non-tracking or non-personalized manner to the extent the SDK and platform allow.

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — EEA/UK (and other consent-required regions as applicable). If a mobile build serves ads in the EEA, the UK, or other regions that require it, we will use a consent message (such as Google’s UMP) so you can accept, reject, or manage non-essential advertising and similar processing before personalized ads are served, consistent with the GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive.

Opt-out paths (applicable if and when mobile ads are enabled):

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and the ATT prompt in the app
  • Android: reset or opt out of ads personalization in device settings (Google’s ads-personalization controls or successor settings)
  • Industry opt-outs as applicable (for example, NAI, DAA, or AppChoices), plus any in-game or store “Your Privacy Choices” link
  • California “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” / “Your Privacy Choices,” to the extent applicable once ad sharing is active

Before any mobile build with advertising ships, we will update this section with the exact ad-network provider name, its privacy-policy link, and the data disclosures published in Apple App Privacy and Google Play Data Safety.

5.3 What we will still not do without a further policy update

Even after mobile IAP or ads go live, the following are not part of the current product plan and would require a further update to this policy before they are introduced: player accounts; cloud save to a Cyber Tuna Labs server; third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs; forced or interstitial ads; and sale of personal information for monetary consideration.

6. Children’s privacy (COPPA and similar rules)

Swarm Genesis is a general-audience game. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, consistent with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act and the FTC’s COPPA Rule, 16 C.F.R. Part 312.

In the EEA and the United Kingdom, the age of digital consent may be as high as 16, depending on the country (GDPR Article 8). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16 in those regions where that is the applicable age.

No final target age rating has been set, and the game is not enrolled in the Google Play Families policy or the Apple Kids Category. A Families / mixed-audience / age-rating decision must be made before mobile-store submission. If the audience or an advertising configuration would bring COPPA or Families obligations into play, this policy and the product will be updated before that version ships.

If you believe a child has provided us personal information (for example, in an email), contact us using the address in Section 11. A parent or guardian may request that we delete personal information about their child that we hold, and we will do so, subject to any limited exception the law allows (for example, retaining a record of the request itself).

  • Disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about them, the sources, the purposes, and the categories of third parties to whom we disclosed it (right to know / access)

Cyber Tuna Labs LLC is a Nevada limited liability company. Nevada law (NRS 603A.300–.360, including NRS 603A.340) gives Nevada consumers the right to submit a verified request to opt out of the sale of certain “covered information” (which can include, among other things, a name, postal address, email address, telephone number, Social Security number, an identifier that allows a specific person to be contacted physically or online, and similar data, as defined in NRS 603A.330).

We do not sell covered information. If that ever changes, we will update this policy before any sale and honor opt-out requests.

8. Retention

  • Local game saves remain on your device until you delete them, clear browser or app storage, or uninstall the game. We do not hold a server-side copy of ordinary play progress.
  • Email and privacy requests are retained as reasonably needed to complete the request, resolve disputes, and meet legal or tax recordkeeping duties, then deleted or de-identified.
  • If IAP receipts are later received from Apple or Google, we will retain only what is needed to deliver, restore, and account for purchases, and for legal or tax retention, and we will state that period when IAP goes live.
  • If you export a JSON save, you control that file.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the current product: progress is stored locally on your device or browser rather than on a Cyber Tuna Labs game server, and we do not currently collect payment-card data. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Browser storage, local files, and email can be lost, intercepted, or accessed if your device or account is compromised. The web preview also depends on itch.io’s and your browser’s security. You are responsible for controlling exported save files and for the security of your own device and email account.
