The studio

Born in Las Vegas

We’re an indie studio out of Sin City making games we actually want to play at 2 a.m. No corporate playbook, no soulless clones — just weird ideas, sharp visuals, and games built to keep you hooked.

Good Vibes, Sharp Edges

If it doesn’t look killer and feel good under your thumbs, it doesn’t ship. We obsess over the juice, the pacing, and the little moments that make you say “okay, one more.”

Whatever Hits Next

No copycat formulas, no templates. Some nights it’s a frantic space shooter, other nights it’s a chill puzzle game — born from late-night studio rabbit holes and player suggestions we couldn’t stop thinking about.

We’re Actually Here

Tell us what slaps and what’s broken. We read it, we fix it fast, and we keep making our games better because keeping you entertained is the whole point.
