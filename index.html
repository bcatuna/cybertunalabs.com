01 About

An independent studio in Las Vegas

Cyber Tuna Labs LLC is an independent game studio and a Nevada limited liability company based in Las Vegas. The studio develops original sci-fi arcade and puzzle games for desktop and mobile and publishes them under the Cyber Tuna Labs name.

Player support, privacy inquiries, and legal inquiries are handled by the company. The public business address is 732 S 6th St Ste N, Las Vegas, NV 89101, United States.

Two original games are underway. Swarm Genesis, a sci-fi arcade and roguelite space shooter, has a free playable web preview. Cosmic Weave, a puzzle game for mobile, is in development. A public build of Cosmic Weave has not been released. Neither game is offered on the Apple App Store or Google Play.
